Troll Ball Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email lmai41010@gmail.com and include your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem.

How do I play?

Hold your phone at a comfortable angle and tap to start. That angle counts as “flat”; tilt away from it to roll the ball. Reach the checkered finish line at the top without rolling into the black.

The ball doesn’t move

The ball only moves when you tilt the phone away from the angle you held it at when you tapped start. Tilt a bit further. If it still doesn’t respond, go back to the menu and start the level again.

What are the strange tiles?

  • Cracked tiles collapse shortly after you roll onto them — keep moving.
  • Purple tiles (⇄) swap left and right.
  • Tiles with ↔ slide side to side and carry the ball.
  • Some tiles vanish or appear as you get close, and not every finish line is real. Good luck.

Revive and skip

After you fall you can revive at your last safe spot, or skip a level you’re stuck on (after three tries), by watching a short optional ad. Players who bought Remove Ads revive and skip without ads.

I bought Remove Ads on another device / reinstalled the game

Open the menu and tap Restore purchases (below the Remove ads button), signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.
